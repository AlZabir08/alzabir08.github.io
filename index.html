Research Interest

My research interests include robotics, machine learning and cyber-physical systems, with an emphasis on sensor fusion and computational modeling. Ongoing projects explore physics-informed machine learning for digital twins and vision-based autonomous robotics, aiming to bridge the gap between physical environments and intelligent algorithms.

I am open to collaborative projects and graduate opportunities related to intelligent systems and robotics. A diverse background in embedded hardware prototyping, computer vision and predictive health monitoring further strengthens my ability to contribute to interdisciplinary research and development initiatives.

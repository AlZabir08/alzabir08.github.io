Research

Physics-Informed Digital Twins for Micro-Mechatronic Systems

This study looks at how a digital model can track a small motor system. It combines a maths-based model with readings from several sensors. The data helps track the system and predict changes in heat. We also studied how certain the model’s predictions were.

Manuscript in revision · Journal of Intelligent Manufacturing

Vision-Based Crack Detection and Coverage Planning

This study combines crack detection with route planning for a mobile robot. A camera model finds cracks while the robot follows a zig-zag path. Infrared and camera readings help the robot avoid getting stuck. The method reached 94.17% coverage in a Webots simulation.

Manuscript in revision · International Journal of Intelligent Robotics and Applications

Vision Transformer-Based Damage Assessment

We used satellite images to study building damage after Hurricane Harvey. A model called ViT-B/32 reached 97.85% accuracy in our tests. It performed better than the CNN model used for comparison. Heatmaps showed which parts of each image influenced the result.

2026 · Journal publication

Machine Learning-Based Hepatitis C Detection

We compared ten machine learning models for detecting Hepatitis C. The models used clinical data to make their predictions. AdaBoost gave the highest accuracy among the models we tested. The study explored how these tools could support automated detection.

2025 · Conference publication

Smartphone-Based Physiological Monitoring

This study used a phone camera to record changes in blood flow. We processed these PPG signals with machine learning models. The models estimated blood pressure, blood oxygen, stress, and heart health risks. The aim was to study health monitoring with a mobile device.

2025 · Conference publication

Machine Learning-Based Prediction of Nightmares in Children

We studied how machine learning could help predict nightmares in children. The work brought data analysis into child mental health research. It focused on using data to support psychological assessment. The study was presented at COMPAS 2025.

2025 · Conference publication

Stress Detection Using PPG Signals

We studied how PPG signals could help detect stress. Machine learning models looked for patterns in these signals. The aim was to assess stress using data from the body. The research was presented at QPAIN 2025.

2025 · Conference publication

YOLOv8-Based Vehicle Detection and Classification

We used YOLOv8 to find and classify vehicles in traffic footage. The study focused on roads in Bangladesh. It explored how real-time video could support traffic monitoring. We presented the work at ICRPSET 2024 in Rajshahi.
